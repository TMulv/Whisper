## Root Cause Hypothesis

**Two layered causes, both grounded in evidence above:**

1. **Bridge-level (CR-01):** The 1000 ms `_lastProgrammaticNavMs` window in `epubBridgeHtml.ts:172` is not a reliable proxy for "this is the initial render." On slower devices or larger EPUBs the first `locationChanged` arrives after 1 s and is mis-tagged `programmatic:false`. The React-side gate trusts the flag and lets the chapter-0 CFI overwrite the user's saved position via the debounced save in `useEpubPosition.ts:23-31`. **This is the dominant failure mode and matches the user's exact report.**

2. **Architectural (A4):** Even with the bridge fixed, the position-restore correctness rests on four writers and three gates having to agree across an asynchronous 3-30 s window. The four post-close `fix(position):` commits demonstrate that this surface keeps producing new races. The user's proposed simplification — single writer, single trigger set, single conflict rule (`max(positionA, positionB)`) — eliminates the entire class of races, not just the current instance.

The user's experience ("never remembers where I left off") is therefore expected given the current code, and the proposed refactor is the correct response. The diagnostics do **not** support continuing the patch-on-patch approach: each of the last four commits closed a window and exposed another.
