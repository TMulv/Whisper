What Moe Can Do

ONE APP.
TWO FORMATS.

Read with your eyes or listen with your ears — import your .epub and .m4b files and Gno Moe handles the rest.

📖
EPUB Reader
Full EPUB3 support with custom fonts, themes, and reflow. A comfortable reading experience for any book in your library.
🎧
M4B Player
Chapter-aware audiobook playback with variable speed, sleep timer, and bookmark support for every M4B file.
⇄
Instant Handoff
Switch from listening to reading (or back) at any moment. Moe remembers your position and drops you in exactly the right place.
📡
Cross-Device
Your position, bookmarks, and library sync across every device you own. Pick up exactly where you left off.

Offline First
Everything lives on-device. No network needed to read, listen, or sync — your books are always available.
🔍
In-Book Search
Full-text search across EPUB chapters and audio transcripts. Jump to any passage instantly.

HOW THE
SYNC WORKS

01
Import Your Files
Drop in your .epub and .m4b files. Gno Moe matches them automatically by title, or you pair them manually.
02
Alignment Builds
Gno Moe maps your audio chapters to your EPUB chapters in the background — quietly, while you read or listen.
03
Switch Freely
Tap Read or Listen at any moment. Moe converts your position and picks up exactly where you were — no chapter hunting.
04
Stay in Sync Everywhere
Your position syncs to all your devices instantly. Continue on your phone, iPad, or any platform Gno Moe runs on.
